# Logia

Logia helps adults learn mathematics by identifying prerequisite knowledge gaps and guiding their learning.

## Language

**Learner**:
An adult pursuing a mathematics learning goal through Logia.

**Learning goal**:
The mathematical understanding or ability a learner wants to develop, clarified from their requested topic.

**Prerequisite**:
Knowledge or a skill needed to learn another mathematical concept or skill.

**Diagnostic quiz**:
A short, adaptive set of questions used to gather tentative evidence of a learner's understanding, including their grasp of prerequisites. Diagnosis can continue during learning.

**Knowledge gap**:
A missing or insufficient understanding relevant to a learner's goal. Quiz evidence can suggest a gap without establishing it with certainty.

**Learning plan**:
A sequence of learning activities intended to address a learner's knowledge gaps and advance their learning goal.

**Learning resource**:
Material a learner can use to develop understanding, including external lessons and explanations provided within Logia.

**Skill**:
A specific mathematical ability that can be assessed, such as adding fractions with unlike denominators.

**Topic**:
A broad grouping of mathematical concepts and skills, such as algebra, used to navigate learning.

**Curriculum graph**:
The shared map of mathematical concepts and skills and their prerequisite relationships.
_Avoid_: Learner knowledge graph

**Learner knowledge model**:
The evidence-based, uncertain picture of an individual learner's understanding of the skills in the curriculum graph. An unassessed skill is distinct from an identified knowledge gap.
_Avoid_: Learner knowledge graph, mastery record

**Learning evidence**:
Observations of a learner applying mathematical understanding that inform the learner knowledge model. Completing a resource alone does not establish understanding.

**Resource completion**:
A record that a learner finished a learning resource, distinct from evidence that they can apply its ideas.

**Supported practice**:
Work a learner completes with hints or other assistance. It contributes learning evidence but is distinct from demonstrating independent understanding.

**Independent check**:
Questions a learner attempts without hints to demonstrate that they can apply a skill, including beyond a worked example.

**Readiness**:
An evidence-based estimate that a learner understands a skill sufficiently to progress. It requires successful independent work across multiple questions and remains open to revision.

**Retention check**:
A later assessment that gathers fresh evidence about a previously demonstrated skill. Elapsed time alone does not establish a knowledge gap.
